    Claude Figma GitHub Pages Prototype

    WiseTransact

    Most people's financial year already lives in their SMS inbox — WiseTransact turns it into a CA-ready PDF
    Read

    Freelancers and salaried professionals with multiple income streams spend hours reconstructing their financial year from memory, bank portals, and forwarded messages — then hand their CA a half-built spreadsheet. The insight was simple: the data isn't missing, it's in SMS. WiseTransact reads those messages, categorizes inflows and outflows, and exports a clean, structured PDF your CA can work with immediately.

    Owned end-to-end: identified the problem, defined the output before designing the input, built brand guidelines and a typography system in Figma, prototyped the working product with Claude, and shipped via GitHub Pages.

    View demo View PRD
  • 02
    Claude Figma GitHub Pages Prototype

    Zepto Cafe

    Increasing repeat order rate from 12% to 25%
    Read

    Zepto Cafe's growth was capped by a low repeat order rate of 12% — first-time users tried the product but rarely came back. The goal was to lift that to 25% by understanding what breaks the second-order habit and designing interventions across discovery, ordering, and post-order re-engagement.

    Owned end-to-end: framed the retention problem, mapped the repeat-order journey, designed the experience in Figma, and prototyped the working flow with Claude — shipped via GitHub Pages.
